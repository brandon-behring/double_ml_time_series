# What the remaining treatment variation identifies

A low prediction error answers an observable question. To interpret a residual slope causally, specify the intervention, comparison, population, horizon, information available before assignment, and the identifying variation left after conditioning. In the binary conditionally unconfounded case, a common partially linear slope averages conditional effects with weights proportional to the conditional treatment variance. If effect sizes differ by history, changing assignment probabilities changes those weights. A known-probability, population-standardized ATE answers a different question. [Vansteelandt and Dukes](https://academic.oup.com/jrsssb/article/84/3/657/7056162) · [Repo estimands by study](study-inventory.md)

## Conditioning on observation

In a 400-person balanced assignment control, everyone has the same outcome under either treatment. Half the participants in each arm have baseline outcome −1 and half +1. Retain high-outcome people in the treated arm and low-outcome people in control. **Exactly 50% are retained in both arms; the observed contrast is +2 while the true effect is zero.** The calculation isolates the selection mechanism using known potential outcomes. [Executable control](reproduction/design_probes.py) · [Measured values](reproduction/design-probes.json)

In Bangalore, GPS availability is 76.0% for high charges and 75.7% for control. Those numbers are helpful for auditing data quality but cannot identify the effect on unobserved travel days. The original daily fixed-effects coefficient and later month-level participant averages also weight people differently. In London, restricting to 982 enrollees present in all 48 events barely changes the observed effect, which supports robustness to that restriction. Neither field dataset reveals what the missing outcomes would have been. A Lee-type bound would require a defensible monotone selection mechanism and changes the target to an always-observed group. ## How to read a successful reproduction

Matching an author estimate evaluates coding, sample definition, units and analysis choices to the precision available. Matching a competing package with identical nuisance predictions tests score algebra. Neither supplies the unobserved counterfactual for a live observational study. Use a known assignment, planted response or credible identifying design as the next evidence layer. Where neither arm appears at a required clock time, report the comparison as unsupported. Where both appear but the interval is wide, report uncertainty and its size.
