# Which question does each study actually answer?

A unit in this inventory is a scientific question on a dataset. Several questions share an assignment or outcome history. The ten-chapter book, the web edition and a script replaying a printed table can all report the same evidence. The 40 named application families below reconcile the 36-family [earlier inventory](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/adversarial/applications.md) with four additions. The six suites contain 40 **commands** by coincidence. A compound command and an application family can each have several targets. The table names them explicitly. The runner names in backticks can be supplied to the [registry](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/external/run.py).

## Randomized assignments, follow-ups and transfer

| Study and runnable evidence | Unit, timing and quantity | Benchmark, decisive check and remaining limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Cobb County water · `water` | Household letter before summer, later household consumption. Peer letter versus control is one contrast in a multiarm experiment. | Compare arm means, interacted ANCOVA and ridge on identical households. Two earlier bills explain much of the available precision gain. A single SE supplies no coverage experiment. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/adversarial/empirical.md) |
| Michigan turnout · `turnout` | Households assigned before one election, voters supply turnout. | Compare neighbor mailing and control using household-grouped folds and clustered SEs. Count 119,999 assigning households in the matched comparison. More voters per assigned household change the variance calculation. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/adversarial/empirical.md) |
| Trondheim iFlex · `electricity-shifting` and pilot | Randomized enrolled households, deliberately scheduled peak events. Peak load, next-day load and 48-hour total answer different questions. | Use same homes and event windows, compare baseline subtraction and ridge. Test rebound on total energy. Household assignment does not randomize each calendar event. [Energy analysis](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/questions/energy.md) |
| HeartSteps · standalone R pilot | Available person-decisions, log steps in 30 minutes. Recorded assignment and actual delivery are distinct. | Reproduce author WCLS and person-level uncertainty. Removing the first 960 decisions changes the study-time mix. Check assignment coding and post-window missingness before comparing folds. [Pilot](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/pilots/heartsteps.md) |
| Drink Less · `drink-less`, `reminder-habituation` | Person-evening assignment to a notification, subsequent app opening. | Separate risk ratio, risk difference, and early-to-late difference. Hold outcome scale fixed, cluster by person. A declining proximal response does not identify accumulated notification exposure. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/questions/reminders.md) |
| myBPmyLife · `mybp`, `reminder-cohorts`, `rolling-reminders`, `shrinkage-replay` | Participants repeatedly randomized to a prompt; immediate steps, contrasts for new entrant cohorts, forecasts issued at successive origins. | Score against logged assignment and mature step outcomes. Compare zero, prior mean, shrinkage and ridge AIPW on common later people. Jointly refit across shared origins. [Rolling evidence](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/rolling/reminders.md) |
| Israeli day-care fines · `daycare-fines` | Six of ten centers assigned a fine, repeated weekly late pickups. | Enumerate all 210 assignments. After simultaneous repeal the contrast concerns the entire prior treatment history. Center-weeks add observations within an assignment. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/questions/daycare.md) |
| Pennsylvania bonus · `pennsylvania-bonus` | Claimant offers in early and later cohorts; first full subsequent quarter of UI earnings. | Compare an earlier frozen offer effect with the later randomized contrast. Change wage-release lag and inspect which offices survive the cutoff. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/forward/pennsylvania.md) |
| JTPA · `jtpa-holdout` | Offer to an adult trainee; observed UI earnings among later participants at different sites. | Compare offer effects, site adjustment and historical AIPW. Keep original and corrected earnings decoders, and compare on shared sites. A changed site mix and unobserved wages limit transfer. [Holdout and corrections](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/rolling/jtpa.md) |
| Canvassing · `canvassing-elections` | Randomized attempted canvass before an election; later cities share an election date. | Score the offer-effect forecast against each later ITT. Actual visits involve compliance. A small pooled miss can hide city-level errors. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/forward/canvassing.md) |
| PROGRESA · `progresa-schooling` | Villages assigned early access; child schooling after the rollout. | Separate published forecasting exercise and randomized enrollment contrast. Timestamp each input and distinguish the working-paper model from the final specification. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/forward/progresa.md) |
| Kenya fertilizer · `fertilizer-timing` | Two cohorts of household offers in 2004; adoption ascertained later. | Verify whether first-cohort outcomes matured before the second assignment. Both assignments preceded the 2005 measurement. The comparison can test cohort differences retrospectively. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/forward/fertilizer.md) |
| Microcredit · `microcredit-sites` | Seven trials, profits standardized to a fortnight. Programs, participants and sites vary. | Leave-one-site-out contrasts assess geographic transfer. Trial dates and unit conversion govern claims about forecasting a future site. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/forward/microcredit.md) |
| Ban the Box · `ban-the-box` | Randomized applicant name signal, employer callback, observational policy introduction. | Score the randomized name contrast in each wave after callbacks mature. The policy effect requires additional between-place/time assumptions. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/forward/ban-box.md) |
| Indonesia teacher pay · `teacher-pay` | Randomized acceleration of certification by school; learning at two follow-ups. | Evaluate a paired change using whole schools. The same schools at follow-up measure persistence. Joint uncertainty matters even when each separate interval includes zero. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/forward/teacher-pay.md) |
| Michigan weatherization · `weatherization` | Household encouragement; applications, audits, retrofits and forecast savings. | Public data identify offer effects on take-up. Compare engineering savings projections with their stated definitions. Restricted bills prevent an independent test of realized savings. [Data boundary](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/forward/weatherization.md) |
| Retail recommendations · `retail-recommendations`, `rolling-controls` | Logged recommendation policies on shared days, item exposures, clicks. | Compare direct randomized policy traffic, the value estimated by importance weighting and doubly robust scores, and a forecast of later lift. Check actions with tiny support and distinguish data clocks. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/forward/retail-recommendations.md) |
| Repeated effort experiment · `rolling-controls` | Independent workers in 2015 and 2018, fifteen common incentives. | Estimate rank preservation separately from differences in effect size. Replicate with identical arm and reference definitions. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/rolling/external-controls.md) |
| Sierra Leone development · `rolling-controls` | One village rollout, repeated infrastructure and institutional outcomes. | Check a common-item index, control-group standardization and arm-based imputation. Interpret persistence conditional on the original intervention history. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/rolling/external-controls.md) |
| Nudge units · `rolling-controls` | Programs in successive annual portfolios, multiple arms per trial. | A trial-weighted later error and an arm-weighted published mean answer different questions. Year-end availability is assumed when exact release dates are missing. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/rolling/external-controls.md) |
| US GSS · `gss-forms` | Questionnaire forms assigned within survey waves, change in giving the “too little” answer. | Use same contrast and survey weights. Historical wave errors can be measured. The issued future forecast remains pending until actual assignments and outcomes exist. [Issuance record](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/prospective/gss.md) |
| International cricket · `cricket-toss` | Toss winners earn the choice of innings order; matches in calendar blocks. | Distinguish the effect of winning the toss from choosing to bat. Preserve frozen annual and quarterly targets and series dependence. Inspect precision using historical assignments. [Issuance record](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/prospective/cricket.md) |

## Observational, quasi-experimental and forecast comparisons

| Study and runnable evidence | Unit, timing and quantity | Benchmark, decisive check and remaining limitation |
|---|---|---|
| US 401(k) · standalone R and Python | 1991 households; eligibility in PLR, eligibility ATE in an interactive model, participation LATE under the eligibility instrument. | Compare like panels, nuisance predictions and partition aggregation. The author script’s “Best” output selected the same interactive result as lasso. The original partition RNG was unavailable. [Published targets and selection](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/07-401k-replication.md) |
| Dominick’s orange juice · `retail-timing` and standalone refit | Store–brand–week price and sales; separately promotion and subsequent volumes. | Compare conditional slopes on identical rows under brand controls and store/week variance. Pricing and promotion respond to demand. No randomized household demand elasticity is observed. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/questions/retail.md) |
| Washington bike share · standalone pilot | Daily weather associations and a known intervention planted on the observed rental series. | Distinguish observational weather effects from the planted effect with recorded assignment law. Compare same-day known-probability and temporal estimators for bias and coverage. [Pilot](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/pilots/bike.md) |
| California cigarettes · standalone Python/R | One policy adoption in one state; annual per-capita sales and donor states. | Compare synthetic DiD, donor weights and pre-fit. A temporal fold with no earlier treated dates cannot empirically learn treatment response from that training set. [Pilot](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/pilots/california.md) |
| Italian bank capital · `italy` | GDP response two quarters after a normalized bank-capital shock. | Reproduce published reverse-time design, then compare local projections and forward fits on common calendar-valid quarters. The shock, its normalization and small effective sample determine interpretation. [Matched reanalysis](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/adversarial/empirical.md) |
| Sicily smoking ban · `sicily` | One intervention date in an aggregate rate series. | Replay segmented time-series models, seasonality and count/exposure definition. Concurrent events and the counterfactual trend remain identifying assumptions. [Replication](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/11-external-replication-benchmarks.md) |
| US fiscal multipliers · `ramey`, `classical-econometrics` | Military news instrument, cumulative spending and GDP at eight and sixteen quarters. | Compare exactly identified IV, author-data normalization and automatic Bartlett SE. Paper entries match at printed precision. Archived results workbook differs. [Classical replay](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/classical/README.md) |
| Minimum wage · `wage-employment` | NJ/PA restaurant survey, then adjacent US counties and commuting zones with policy changes. | Match employment definition, weighted geography and prepolicy trends. These samples and variation cannot be pooled as independent replications of one effect. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/questions/minimum-wage.md) |
| Divorce reform · `wolfers` | Staggered state adoption, divorce rates over event time. | Reproduce original model, examine calendar/cohort composition and alternative event-time comparisons. Passing a low-power pretrend check cannot establish parallel untreated trends. [Panel replay](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/external/panels/README.md) |
| State beer taxes · `beer` | Tax components and consumption over time in states. | Reproduce published units, fixed effects and state-clustered variance. Plausibility of untreated counterfactual and cross-border substitution remain empirical issues. [Replication](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/11-external-replication-benchmarks.md) |
| Movies and assaults · `dahl` | Weekend attendance and assaults; attendance instrumented using the study design. | Check instrument, timing, first stage and outcome transformation. A precision gain in forecasting attendance is insufficient to establish exclusion. [Panel replay](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/external/panels/README.md) |
| Bracero exclusion · `bracero-wages` | State exposure interacted with one national change; farm wages. | Compare preperiod placebo dates, jointly calibrated inference and stability to exposure definitions. Calendar splits do not create additional policy terminations. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/forward/bracero.md) |
| Fulton fish market · standalone published-data calculation | Whiting demand on 111 observed trading days, storms as an instrument for price. | Compare the first stage, IV demand slope and reduced form on each set of trading days. The last block contains no storm dates. Contrast unavailable there. [Source and estimates](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/06-public-datasets.md#fulton-fish-market--a-short-demand-series-with-an-instrument) |
| US GDP releases · `gdp-vintages` | Outcome forecast at historical survey deadlines; first release supplies the realized target. | Compare vintages, lag rules and actual publication timestamps. RMSE here evaluates an observable outcome forecast. [Vintage replay](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/forward/gdp.md) |

## Additions since the original 36-family review

| Study and runnable evidence | Assignment and target | Decisive check |

| National Supported Work · `classical-econometrics` | Randomized program, 1978 earnings in the specified 185/260-person experimental sample. | Match $1,794.34 unadjusted under both SE conventions and common-fold predictions against DoubleML. Preserve the randomized control sample. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/classical/README.md) |
| Grunfeld investment · `classical-econometrics` | Observational firm–year investment conditional on value, capital and firm effects, ten-firm textbook subset. | Within and full dummy coefficients should coincide on the same rows. Permute and unbalance the panel. This verifies projection algebra. [Results](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/classical/README.md) |
| Bangalore commuting · `bangalore-pricing` | Assigned commuter arms; Table I weights commuter-days, rolling weighted changes give one vote to a commuter within each arm/month before weights. | Match published IV-free fixed-effects regression and cluster SE, count both assigned commuters and observable days, inspect GPS selection. | London electricity · `london-tariffs` | Day-ahead event prices shared by opting-in households. Annual target weights start-time slots equally. | Count days, compare documented clock and alternative joins. Q4 has no matched start hour. ## Other scientific and software evidence

| Evidence | What is evaluated |
|---|---|
| `classical-impulse`, `paired-time-splits` | Known exogenous impulse responses and matched splits on known effects. The break, hidden-cause, oracle covariance and later-block forecasts are distinct checks sharing generated paths. [Paired designs](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/paired/README.md) |
| `rolling-calibration`, `rolling-bootstrap` | Stable, declining and composition-changing treatment effects; correlated alarms across origins and repeated use of the same participants. [Calibration design](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/rolling/calibration.md) |

| Chapters 4–6 | Retail conditional slope, scalar temporal PLR and clustered panel effects. Audit FE transformation, date/worker folds, event time, outcome maturity and the score clock for variance. | Chapters 7–10 and appendix | FRED macro controls and their release vintages, simulated insurance outcomes and units, conditional effects and policy decisions, operational monitoring, and the proposed Julia implementation. [Teaching inventory](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/03-manuscript-and-content.md) |
| Archived validation tables, sensitivity, storage, monitoring, registry and standalone scripts | Preserve frozen historical inputs/results. [Standalone replay](standalone-reproduction.md) · [Corrections](corrections.md) |
| Literature and candidate datasets | The 158-paper journal screen examined access and timing metadata. It cannot estimate how often published causal conclusions were wrong. Prospective or screened candidates without an executable effect claim are source leads. [Journal screening rules](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/forward/journals.md) · [Candidate dataset ledger](../2026-11-19_time_series_dml/06-public-datasets.md) |
