A full corrected comparison refitted 20 datasets in each of six configurations with seven methods. Controlled OLS again had the smallest mean absolute bias (0.03388, against 0.05021 for DML) and the smallest MSE (0.01563, against 0.11972). This fresh-run relative bias reduction was **−48.17%**. The replay of the old reporting code declared a 70.7% reduction on the fresh aggregates by comparing DML with the worst baseline. Its different magnitude from the fixed-archive rescore is expected because the new fits use the current validators and data-seed handling. Mean marginal coverage was 66.7% for controlled OLS and 77.5% for DML; neither figure warrants a nominal-95% claim. The 20-draw design gives limited precision for comparisons of small biases.
