# How much hindsight enters a GDP forecast through revisions?

Across 104 survey deadlines from 2000Q1 through 2025Q4, an expanding autoregression had a root mean squared error of **5.559 annualized percentage points** against first-released next-quarter GDP growth. Giving the same model the later revisions to the same historical quarters lowered that error to **5.394**. Its mean absolute error fell by **0.055 points**, with a paired 95% interval of **−0.168 to +0.059** for revised minus available inputs. The professional survey had a first-release RMSE of **4.549**. These are prediction errors for one US series. A causal policy effect would require an intervention and a comparison. **Forecast and target.** Survey field `DRGDP3` is annualized growth computed from consecutive median forecast levels. Its target is the quarter *after* the survey quarter, called H=2 in the Fed tables. The code scores the median forecast against the first public growth estimate. As an independent check, for the Fed's separate sample of 105 **target** quarters from 1997Q1 to 2023Q1, the replay gives mean actual minus forecast **−0.37488**, MAE **2.02175**, and RMSE **4.53551**. These round to the published −0.37, 2.02 and 4.54. [SPF definitions](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/-/media/frbp/assets/surveys-and-data/survey-of-professional-forecasters/spf-documentation.pdf) · [Published error statistics, table 1A](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/-/media/FRBP/Assets/Surveys-And-Data/survey-of-professional-forecasters/data-files/RGDP/SPF_Error_Statistics_RGDP_3_AICt.txt)

**What could be known.** Panelists and public analysts could use the GDP releases available by each survey deadline. The survey operator could receive confidential submissions at the deadline. Public analysts obtained the published median on the subsequent news-release date. At the February 10, 2009 deadline, 2008Q4 growth computed from the then-public levels was **−3.804%**. The October 29, 2026 vintage gives **−8.473%** for that same quarter. The archive records the two survey dates, while individual submission times and preliminary medians are unavailable. The AR predictions use public data at the deadline and the published SPF result serves as the external forecast of that target. On November 11, 2025, the complete public series ended at 2025Q2. The AR recursively projected through 2026Q1, predicting **2.664%**. The survey was published November 17 at **1.569%** and the initial target growth was **1.990%**. BEA first announced the delayed 2025Q3 and 2025Q4 observations on December 23, 2025 and February 20, 2026. The script reconstructs their annualized rates of **4.343%** and **1.423%** from consecutive levels within each release vintage. [Deadlines and release dates](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/-/media/FRBP/Assets/Surveys-And-Data/survey-of-professional-forecasters/spf-release-dates.txt) · [Survey timing](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/surveys-and-data/real-time-data-research/spf-faqs) · [Q3 release](https://www.bea.gov/news/2025/gross-domestic-product-3rd-quarter-2025-initial-estimate-and-corporate-profits) · [Q4 release](https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/gdp-advance-estimate-4th-quarter-and-year-2025)

**How the comparison works.** The expanding `temporalcv` split advances one survey origin at a time. Each fit uses an intercept, one lag and the contiguous quarterly growth history from 1992Q1 through the last observation actually available. The hindsight fit uses October 2026 revisions over precisely that date range. The interval for the change in absolute error uses quarterly paired loss differences, a Bartlett kernel, and four lags. Both forecasts are re-scored on identical targets against the revised levels. The Fed's historical autoregression benchmarks use their own rolling specifications. ## Reproduce

The source manifest pins all 107 dated GDP responses, covering 104 deadlines, two supplemental first releases and the later evaluation vintage. The 117-row survey extract covers the union of evaluation and published-reference origins. The optional flag compares the extract with the original deadline list and both live Fed workbooks. It ignores appended future survey rows and uses separate release dates for the two shutdown targets. The command emits the forecasts, cutoff dates, and both outcomes for each scored origin. [Source checksums](../external/forward/gdp-vintages.csv) · [Archived survey cells](../external/forward/gdp-survey.csv) · [Tests](../../../../test/integration/test_forward_economy.py)

```bash
audit=docs/audits/2026-11-19_time_series_dml
uv run --locked python "$audit/external/forward/gdp.py" \
  --cache-dir "${XDG_CACHE_HOME:-$HOME/.cache}/dml-ts/external" --verify-survey
```
