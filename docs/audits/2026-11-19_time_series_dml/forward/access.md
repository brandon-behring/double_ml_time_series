# Which data could an analyst obtain, and when?

Access below is specific to the outcome and comparison. A public copy today establishes reproducibility today. The two rightmost columns record the historical evidence required to call an exercise prospective. A journal's entire print roster is the denominator for screening even when the necessary data remain restricted. [Article-level screening and access](journals.md)

| Study and verified material | Availability at the decision or forecast cutoff | Public analyst at that cutoff |
|---|---|---|
| **myBPmyLife**. The hashed MRT decision archive, enrollment dates, baseline data and IDs join at the participant level. [Decision records](https://github.com/WIRED-L-um/MRT_myBPmyLife/tree/fa86c0d44ced3876f4a7351bc2d475fc4249342e) · [Enrollment records](https://github.com/WIRED-L-um/myBPmyLife-Primary-Analysis/tree/f47ce32b896f55b2c7279b35aab790a75f4398b1) | Earlier participants could finish the full eligible window before the freeze. The operator reconstruction allows a day for next-hour records. Historical ingestion times are unavailable. | Public files postdate 2022. Zero is an available benchmark. |
| **US GDP**. Survey deadlines, forecasts, dated ALFRED level vintages, and first-release growth are available without a key. [Fed survey](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/surveys-and-data/rgdp) · [Real-time releases](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/surveys-and-data/real-time-data-research/routput) | Every model input uses the series vintage at the survey deadline. Forecasters send their projections to the survey operator. | Historical GDP releases were public. The published survey median can be scored once released, typically after the deadline. |
| **Pennsylvania bonus**. Original selection dates, office, offer, quarterly wage records and codes are in the public research archive, including the Pennsylvania evaluation report. [Original ZIP](https://www.upjohn.org/sites/default/files/2019-02/PA_ReempBonus.zip) | Agencies recorded the benefit and wage records. Their historical analyst-access dates are unknown. Compare three wage reporting lags. | The present research extract supplies no dated 1989 public microdata release. |
| **Canvassing**. The original public estimate and corrected 1998 archive can be distinguished from six 2001 randomized contrasts. [1999 result](https://www.uvm.edu/~dguber/POLS234/articles/gerber.pdf) · [Corrected archive](https://doi.org/10.60600/YU/ODL44F) · [Later trials](https://doi.org/10.60600/YU/B9TZNZ) | The first experiment’s records existed before the later elections. Its subsequently corrected assignment and linkage fields are a retrospective sensitivity. | +2.25 pp was already published in 1999. |
| **PROGRESA**. The authors’ 2006 ZIP was recovered from a 2015 capture of the original AEA download. It contains Stata household records, Fortran estimation and simulation code, model inputs and documentation. [Author tables](https://economics.sas.upenn.edu/sites/default/files/filevault/working-papers/03-022.pdf) · [Archived AEA download](https://web.archive.org/web/20150921083256id_/https://www.aeaweb.org/aer/data/dec06/20031016_data.zip) | The operator collected a 1997 baseline and subsequent follow-up. Using a model trained without treated follow-up and using a pre-rollout population are distinct requirements. | An estimate publicly released before rollout was not established. |
| **Kenya fertilizer**. Published timing demonstrates that the 2005 survey measured both seasons. The Harvard file inventory is public and has a guestbook for downloading. [Original paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.101.6.2350) · [Microdata inventory](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/YM9F07) | Purchases through the operator and household fertilizer use from any supplier are different outcomes. Use was measured after the second season. | First-season household adoption was also unavailable publicly when season two began. |
| **Microcredit**. Meager’s public R workspace has original profit variables, harmonization factors, study estimates and uncertainties. [Author workspace](https://bitbucket.org/rmeager/aggregate-average-impacts-microcredit/src/be1fe9098d5a27bd5a3a9cf84686087f501b0e3b/) | The relevant holdout is a country. Lending programs and assignment designs differ by site. | Published cross-site data support geographic transfer. Historical release ordering is unverified. |
| **Ban the Box**. Harvard provides dated application and callback records plus the authors’ Stata analysis. [Data and code](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/VPHMNT) | The callback window is eight weeks. Maturity at the law’s effective date differs from maturity at the next application wave. | The available replication deposit postdates the fieldwork. |
| **ZOZOTOWN retail recommendations**. The pinned release contains both policy logs for the full week with item, slot, timestamp, click and logged item probability. [Data revision](https://huggingface.co/datasets/zozonext/open-bandit/tree/90a1283aecd19026caf15a0840af1176a41f6a76) | Earlier click-arrival times are undocumented. A 24-hour margin before November 28, 2019 is primary, with zero- and 48-hour comparisons. | Public release followed the experiment. [Forward forecast and randomized benchmark](retail-recommendations.md) |
| **US bracero exclusion**. The original state wage panel, CPI series and code reproduce the published regression. [Data](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/17M4ZP) | Observations are dated quarterly. Analyzing the published regression requires later wages under the actual policy. The three placebo windows end by 1961Q4. | Original record-release dates are unavailable. The reconstruction uses the 2018 archive. [Wage estimates](bracero.md) |
| **Indonesian teacher pay**. The Stata archive contains the analysis files from two follow-ups and their common school identifiers. [Data](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/MTVM5O) | Ten months separate the close of the first follow-up and the start of the later survey. Exact score availability is undocumented. | The working paper and public files postdate both waves. [Timing and paired comparison](teacher-pay.md) |
| **Michigan weatherization**. Application, audit and completion indicators and engineering summaries are public. [Deposit](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/OJFYSR) | The engineering audit preceded installation. The public status file covers one encouragement campaign. | The public archive postdates fieldwork and documents restrictions on the two monthly billing panels. [Take-up and engineering replay](weatherization.md) |
