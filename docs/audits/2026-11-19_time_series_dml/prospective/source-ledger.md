# Sources for a genuinely new assignment episode

This ledger distinguishes the assignment date, outcome date and public release date. At issuance on November 21, 2026 UTC, two public tracks permit a numerical forecast. A later data release can also describe outcomes that had already happened when a forecast was issued.

| Source | Assignment and endpoint | Status and what would make it scoreable |
|---|---|---|
| Senior international Twenty20 cricket | The toss assigns the right to choose innings order. The endpoint is the focal team's final match win credit. | The fixed window opens December 1, 2026 and closes November 30, 2027. The ICC has announced T20Is within it. Reconcile matches, missing toss records, and cancellations with a fixture ledger. The historical archive explicitly withholds some matches. [ICC schedule](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/india-announce-schedule-for-blockbuster-home-season) · [Archive and coverage](https://cricsheet.org/downloads/) |
| General Social Survey | Assigned forms ask about assistance for poor people or welfare, among other differences. The outcome is recording the response “too little.” | NSF identifies a planned 2028 wave. Its questionnaire, randomization and release determine whether the conditional forecast can be scored. Earlier waves with assignment errors are documented by the survey producer. [NSF funding description](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/general-social-survey-competition/505742/nsf24-537) · [NORC assignment audit](https://gss.norc.org/content/dam/gss/get-documentation/pdf/reports/methodological-reports/MR134%20-%20Ballot%20and%20Form.pdf) |
| ADAPT HeartSteps, NCT06915220 | Randomizing a physical-activity suggestion can be linked to a registered **three-hour** step-count endpoint. The reminder benchmark in this repo measures **one-hour** steps. | Recruitment began May 20, 2025. The registry estimates February 2027 completion and lists individual-data sharing as undecided. Reopen the one-hour comparison if compatible decision-level outcomes and a dated post-freeze cohort become available. A three-hour analysis would require its own historical forecast. [Sponsor's registry record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06915220) |
| Federal small-business procurement outreach | OES randomized messages to eligible firms. Clicks and registration were measured on a prespecified schedule. | The rural-business analysis plan gives September 23, 2026 as its endline. A forecast made now awaiting publication would be blind to a result, but its assignments and endpoint preceded issuance. [OES analysis plan, pp. 1–3](https://oes.gsa.gov/assets/analysis/2605-analysis-plan.pdf) |
| WorkAdvance employment training | Randomized sector-focused training and services with employment and earnings follow-ups. | MDRC's releases describe follow-ups of an earlier assignment. A new independently randomized intake with a documented first-full-quarter wage endpoint and reporting clock could support a fresh labor-program comparison. Program availability and a later follow-up of the original participants each answer other questions. [MDRC study and release history](https://www.mdrc.org/work/projects/workadvance) |

For the next labor or reminder candidate, record eligibility, both assigned arms, randomization date, outcome window, reporting delay, and what arm-level or participant-level data will be available. Select and freeze a compatible historical forecast before the new assignments. If access is insufficient, retain the candidate with its reason. This search identified no verified forthcoming public replication of the JTPA first-quarter earnings contrast or the myBP one-hour effect.
