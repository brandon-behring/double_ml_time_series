# Which estimator belongs in each application?

Each row follows a distinct intervention, outcome or forecasting target through its replays. An earlier publication, repeated partitions, alternate reporting lags, and additional folds can yield several numerical checks from one study. A new causal test requires new identifying variation. The recommendations below concern the named target. Each linked study provides the detailed covariates, timing and variance specification.

## Experiments and forecasts of experimental effects

| Study, population and clock | Identification and method already used | Recommendation and decisive evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Cobb County water. Summer use by households randomized to a conservation letter. | Original multi-arm regression, FWL reproduction and fivefold ridge PLR on the peer/control subset. Two pre-mail billing totals were available. | **Prefer ANCOVA.** Two pre-treatment totals explain most of the precision gain. On the same 83,319 households the robust SE is about 0.17 with ordinary regression and with ridge. [Matched trial fits](empirical.md) |
| Michigan turnout. Neighbors mailing versus no mail before one election. | Mailing randomized by household, outcome measured per voter. The ridge extension retains whole households in each fold and adjusts for six earlier elections. | **Prefer household-clustered ANCOVA.** Both approaches give about +8.1 points with SE 0.32. Treating all 229,444 voters as independently assigned understates uncertainty. [Matched trial fits](empirical.md) |
| Trondheim iFlex. Retained experimental households during realized peak events and the day afterward. | Household program assignment; baseline subtraction, randomized contrasts, ridge adjustment and a separate daily-gap temporal regression. Organizers selected event dates. | **Prefer baseline-adjusted household contrasts.** On seven isolated starts the 48-hour effects were −4.01 kWh with baseline adjustment and −4.09 with ridge. A daily event slope needs additional assumptions about event timing. [First and pooled events](../pilots/iflex.md) · [48-hour analysis](../questions/energy.md) |
| HeartSteps V1. Available participants, 30-minute log steps after each eligible prompt decision. | Repeated randomization, weighted and centered least squares, forward score sensitivities. The first week includes different treatment responses. | **Prefer the assignment-aware proximal effect estimator.** Reproduced +0.1312 (SE 0.0674); forward validation omits 960 early decisions. Match occasions before comparing estimators and cluster by person. [HeartSteps](../pilots/heartsteps.md) |
| Drink Less. App opening after randomized notifications to 349 users across 30 evenings. | Repeated assignments with known probabilities; author risk ratio, arm-specific AIPW for a risk difference, and paired early/late contrasts. | **Prefer the randomized risk contrast on the requested scale.** Absolute benefit declined 8.73 points using assignment weights and 8.77 with adjustment. This comparison does not isolate the effect of prior notifications on habituation. [Trial and decline](../questions/reminders.md) |
| myBPmyLife. Next-hour steps under randomized activity prompts, then effects among newly enrolled cohorts. | Assignment-based proximal contrast, participant-disjoint ridge AIPW, and earlier-only quarterly refits after outcome maturity. | **Prefer the known-probability estimator; use DML as a sensitivity.** Zero had the smallest point loss in all four fully evaluable later quarters. The paired, jointly refitted DML gain over half-history spanned zero. Preserve unavailable origins and whole-person uncertainty. [Effect replay](../prospective/development.md) · [Timing and population](../rolling/reminders.md) |
| Day-care fines. Ten centers, six assigned to introduce a late-pickup fine. | Center-level changes and all 210 assignments of six centers. Repeal was simultaneous. | **Prefer exact randomization inference.** Baseline subtraction estimates +9.22 late pickups per center-week during the fine. Post-repeal differences describe the original assignment history. [Day care](../questions/daycare.md) |
| Pennsylvania reemployment bonus. Next-quarter covered earnings in early and later claimant cohorts. | Earlier and later offer/control differences subject to wage-reporting delay. | **Prefer the randomized offer contrast.** Historical forecast error −$108 [−$517, +$300]. An extra reporting lag leaves only one office with usable early data. Precision and site representation constrain forecasts. [Pennsylvania](../forward/pennsylvania.md) |
| JTPA. Adult training offers and first-full-quarter earnings among observed UI records. | Randomized offer with known probability, historical arm difference, ridge AIPW and site standardization; one later cohort. | **Prefer a site-aware randomized contrast; use DML as a sensitivity.** Earlier AIPW +$138.45 and site adjustment +$137.94. Nearly one-quarter of the later observed cohort came from sites absent in earlier data. [Matched site and wage sensitivity](transport.md) |
| Canvassing. Earlier New Haven assignment to an attempted visit, later turnout in six cities. | Published 1999 offer contrast, later independently randomized contrasts on a shared election date. | **Prefer the published arm contrasts** for testing transfer. The pooled miss was +0.15 pp; cities have heterogeneous errors and a common election clock. Visits received require another design. [Canvassing](../forward/canvassing.md) |
| PROGRESA. Enrollment of Mexican children aged 12–15 after subsidy rollout. | Household behavior model and village-randomized differences. The working-paper fit uses 1998 controls. The final article describes a fit without post-program data. | **Prefer the randomized benchmark** for the realized effect and a structural model for the specified policy forecast. Report precisely which contemporaneous control inputs were available. [PROGRESA](../forward/progresa.md) |
| Kenyan fertilizer. Two rounds of offers and later reported adoption. | Randomized offers, source-date audit. Both 2004 assignments occurred before adoption was measured in 2005. | **Prefer randomized contrasts for historical assignment effects; withhold the proposed forward claim.** First-round labels were unavailable before the next offer. [Fertilizer](../forward/fertilizer.md) |
| Microcredit. Profit per fortnight across seven country trials. | Leave-one-country-out mean of randomized trial effects; programs and locations differ. | **Prefer study-level pooling** for a cross-location benchmark. MAE $18.71 versus $19.72 for zero. Seven geographic holdouts do not establish forecast performance over time. [Microcredit](../forward/microcredit.md) |
| Ban the Box. Employer callbacks by randomized name signal across New Jersey and New York City application waves. | Correspondence experiment for the name signal; observational policy change for the law. Callback ascertainment takes eight weeks. | **Prefer randomized name contrasts; treat the law effect as a separate comparison.** No prelaw applications had completed follow-up by the New Jersey law’s March 1, 2015 effective date. Later fieldwork supplies a feasible evaluation date. [Ban the Box](../forward/ban-box.md) |
| Indonesia teacher pay. Student learning after school-level randomized acceleration of certification. | Randomized school triplets, school-level dependence, paired follow-ups in 2011 and 2012. | **Prefer a design-based, jointly estimated change.** Early minus late −0.0158 SD [−0.0448, +0.0132]. This tests persistence in the original schools. [Teacher pay](../forward/teacher-pay.md) |
| Michigan weatherization. Applications, audits, retrofits and energy savings. | Randomized encouragement identifies take-up. Public engineering records contain projected savings; actual monthly bills are restricted. | **Prefer offer/control differences for take-up; withhold a public-data estimate of realized savings.** Completion rose 4.93 pp. An engineering projection has a different outcome and unit. [Weatherization](../forward/weatherization.md) |
| Online retail recommender. Clicks under two randomized policies, then item-level off-policy evaluation. | Direct policy comparison across days; recorded propensities, importance weighting and doubly robust scores for particular actions. | **Prefer direct randomized policy contrasts** when both arms are observed. Use off-policy estimates as a sensitivity only with adequate support. Later effective sample size was 362 from 1.72 million logged rows. [Policy and item analyses](../forward/retail-recommendations.md) · [Assignment repetition](../rolling/external-controls.md) |
| Repeated effort task. New workers and the same 15 incentives in 2015 and 2018. | Paired published arm means, cross-wave rank correlation. | **Prefer the experimental rank benchmark** for an ordering claim. The reproduced 0.907 correlation permits changes in effect magnitude. [Repeated effort](../rolling/external-controls.md) |
| Sierra Leone community development. Infrastructure and institutions in 236 villages at two follow-ups. | One randomized rollout, paired village indices, common-item sensitivity. | **Prefer paired village-level contrasts.** Development declined 0.094 SD (SE 0.036) on the published index. New survey outcomes from old assignments measure persistence. [Sierra Leone](../rolling/external-controls.md) |
| Nudge units. Take-up in later annual portfolios of randomized interventions. | Average arms within trials, then use earlier trial means to predict later trial effects. | **Prefer trial-level historical pooling as a benchmark.** Gain over zero changed sign across 2016–18. Year-level timestamps, evolving intervention mix and only three transitions limit generalization. [Portfolio forecasts](../rolling/external-controls.md) |
| GSS. Recording “too little” spending under the assistance versus welfare questionnaire form. | Randomized form package, survey-weighted arm means with design variance, cross-fitted AIPW and weighted standardized OLS. One conditional 2028 forecast. | **Prefer the design-weighted arm difference.** Seven chronological development holdouts give RMSEs 3.44, 3.47 and 3.45 pp for history, AIPW and OLS. The 2028 comparison awaits a compatible fielded instrument and release. [Matched alternatives](transport.md) · [Frozen GSS design](../prospective/gss.md) |
| International cricket. Winning the toss gives the focal team a choice of innings order. | Fair toss, win credit per eligible match, design score, prognostic adjustment and arm-specific AIPW; series-clustered uncertainty. | **Prefer the randomized contrast; use pre-trained prognostic adjustment for precision.** In 2025 the pooled model reduced the SE from 3.020 to 2.765 pp. The upcoming year's DML and half-history forecasts differ by 0.073 pp. [Historical tests](transport.md) · [Frozen cricket design](../prospective/cricket.md) |

## Observational applications, policy changes and ordinary forecasts

| Study, population and clock | Identification and method already used | Recommendation and decisive evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 401(k). Cross-sectional eligibility or participation and net financial assets in 1991 SIPP. | The author procedures estimate a conditional eligibility contrast and a separate IV complier effect of participation; companion fits also control for contemporaneous wealth. | **Use DML as a sensitivity** alongside flexible conventional adjustment for the nine-control eligibility analysis. **Withhold a total-effect reading** of the wealth-conditioned fit. Total wealth contains the outcome. An instrumented participation effect additionally needs exclusion, monotonicity and relevance. The 600 splits quantify algorithmic variability on one sample. [Specification audit](../07-401k-replication.md) · [Controls and matched regressions](failures.md) |
| Dominick's orange juice. Store–brand–week prices and units sold; separately, post-promotion volumes. | Conditional pooled and forward PLR, OLS, brand controls, store/week clustering. Price, promotion and expected demand may be jointly determined. | **Withhold the causal own-price elasticity and stockpiling claims.** Describe associations. Matched OLS −1.1697 and ridge PLR −1.1673 are close, while adding brand indicators moves PLR to −2.6289. A demand instrument or randomized prices would address a different identification problem. [Retail slopes](../09-panel-retail-insurance.md) · [Promotion histories](../questions/retail.md) |
| Washington bike rentals. Daily calendar and temperature; separate randomized treatment planted on the real outcome history. | Observational temporal PLR for weather; repeated synthetic assignment with known probabilities and effect for validation. | **Withhold a causal temperature response. Prefer known-probability adjustment for the experiment.** On the same days the null mean error was −153.47 rentals with temporal ridge and −3.66 with the design contrast. HAC cannot remove extrapolation bias. [Bike pilot](../pilots/bike.md) |
| California cigarettes. Proposition 99 in one state, annual per-person sales, 38 donors. | Synthetic DiD with donor diagnostics; temporal PLR on the treated state and donor mean. | **Prefer donor-based synthetic DiD** conditional on credible donors and pre-fit. It gives −15.60 packs [−32.01, +0.80]. PLR gives −34.50 despite an adoption-spanning fold trained entirely on untreated dates. Neither estimate supplies observed counterfactual sales. [California](../pilots/california.md) |
| Italy bank capital. GDP response two quarters after a normalized shock in a short macroeconomic series. | Authors' reverse cross-fitted score, forward ridge and conventional local projections. Pandemic removal changes which observations are legitimate lags and leads. | **Use DML as a sensitivity.** Compare the simple projection on identical target dates. Attribution to training direction requires fixing learner, feature set, inference and scoring support. Shock exogeneity and a stable response are substantive assumptions. [Matched Italy comparison](empirical.md) |
| Sicily smoking ban. Acute coronary event rates after the January 2005 intervention. | One regional intervention, 59 months, segmented quasi-Poisson model with population offset, time trend and seasonality. | **Prefer the specified interrupted time series** conditional on a credible trend and absence of coincident shocks. Verify controls where available. The replay gives RR 0.885; a fold at adoption has no treated training observations. [Sicily replay](../11-external-replication-benchmarks.md) |
| US fiscal multipliers. Military spending news as an instrument for cumulative spending and output. | Quarterly historical IV local projections, lagged controls. | **Prefer the structural IV projection** for this shock. Verify the first stage, shock dating and horizon definition. The linear two- and four-year multipliers and HAC SEs reproduce the four published values, 0.66 (0.067) and 0.71 (0.044). The separate archived output workbook differs in both coefficients and SEs. Its generating sample or data version remains unidentified. [Fiscal replay](../classical/README.md#historical-fiscal-multipliers) |
| Minimum wages and restaurant employment. NJ/PA survey plus neighboring US counties and commuting zones. | Original comparisons and forward linear/forest change regressions. Geographic comparison and duplicated policy units influence results. | **Prefer design-specific matched comparisons; use DML as a sensitivity.** Changing the comparison market moved the level elasticity from +0.016 to −0.141. Inspect pretrends, spillovers and policy clustering. [Minimum wages](../questions/minimum-wage.md) |
| US divorce reforms. State law adoption and event-time divorce outcomes. | Population-weighted state/year FE, alternative state trends and native partial regression. | **Prefer a cohort-by-event-time DiD with interpretable comparison states** for heterogeneous timing effects, alongside the published specification. The late coefficient flips sign with state trends. Reproduction establishes correct weighting. [Wolfers replay](../11-external-replication-benchmarks.md) · [Group-time effects](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jeconom.2020.12.001) |
| State beer taxes. Excise and sales tax changes and beer consumption over time. | Published first differences and year effects, conventional and state-clustered uncertainty. | **Prefer the first-difference design with explicit policy assumptions.** Distinguish the paper's stated clustering from the code's conventional SE. The separate retail microdata were withheld. [Beer-tax replay](../11-external-replication-benchmarks.md) |
| US movie audiences and assaults. Weekend attendance and contemporaneous assault counts. | Calendar/weather adjustment and IV using attendance on another weekend. | **Prefer the original IV and reduced-form sensitivity** if timing and exclusion are defensible. Added controls reverse the strong-violence slope. Future weekend attendance is an unavailable input for a real-time forecast. [Movie replay](../11-external-replication-benchmarks.md) |
| Bracero program. State farm wages after national exclusion interacted with prepolicy exposure. | Differential-exposure state/quarter FE and earlier placebo dates. | **Prefer exposure-based DiD** for a relative effect with a justified parallel-trends comparison. The state contrast per ten points of exposure is −0.36 cents [−1.21, +0.50]. A national common effect is absorbed. [Bracero study](../forward/bracero.md) |
| Fulton fish. Price and quantity of whiting, 111 dated trading days. | Weekday-adjusted OLS and storm IV. Last of five blocks contains zero stormy days. | **Prefer IV with weak-instrument diagnostics** subject to a credible exclusion restriction. OLS −0.563 and IV −1.119 need not estimate the same object. Withhold a last-block standalone IV effect. [Fish data and design](../06-public-datasets.md#fulton-fish-market--a-short-demand-series-with-an-instrument) |
| US GDP releases. First-released growth at 104 actual survey deadlines. | Real-time AR(1) and professional forecasts using the appropriate release vintages. No intervention specified. | **Prefer real-time forecasting tools** for forecast error. Later revisions improve apparent historical RMSE from 5.56 to 5.39. This tests feature availability and prediction. [GDP vintages](../forward/gdp.md) |

## Data screens and evidence boundaries

The Kenyan cash-transfer archive check established the 1,586 market-month sample and detected 183 price-index records predating their market's first survey. Estimating geographic spillovers requires the randomized transfer design, treatment distances, timing and interference model. The Los Angeles transit strike, Oregon health lottery, London tariff trial, Opower replications, and the search for new labor/reminder cohorts were design or data-access reviews. Their published coefficients are external evidence. No local DML fit in these screens establishes a method ranking. [Data checks](../06-public-datasets.md) · [Design screens](../questions/applicability.md) · [Opower review](../05-literature.md) · [Prospective sources](../prospective/source-ledger.md)

The screen of 158 empirical papers in three 2018 journal volumes classifies headline claims and data access. It does not verify which historical effects were causally identified. A study that estimates a historical policy effect can answer its original question without ever forecasting a later effect. [Journal screen and paper ledger](../forward/journals.md)
