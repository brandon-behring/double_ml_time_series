# When does double machine learning earn its place?

**November 21, 2026. Use DML when the design already identifies the target and learning a difficult adjustment function materially improves estimation.** The clearest observed benefit here was recovering a known slope in simulations with an unmodeled interaction. In the two large randomized field trials, ordinary regression adjustment achieved essentially the same precision as ridge DML. Across later experimental cohorts, this project has yet to show a reliable advantage in forecasting changes in effects.

## What changed the verdict

| Question | Matched evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Can flexible adjustment recover a causal effect that simpler regressions miss? | On 500 serially dependent datasets with an interaction, tree DML cut RMSE from **0.242** for additive splines to **0.060**. Its nominal 95% intervals covered only **85.6%** of the time. Giving spline regression the true interaction after examining the results yielded RMSE **0.049** and coverage **93.8%**. With smooth additive confounding, the original spline and tree RMSEs were **0.049 and 0.050**. | **Tree DML is a defensible primary point estimator when the interaction is unknown.** Its advantage over an additive specification warrants flexible adjustment; the feature of cross-fitting itself is tested below. Prefer a well-specified regression when available, and separately calibrate interval coverage. The known-interaction sensitivity had information a real analyst would need to justify. [3,000 simulated datasets and paired uncertainty](simulation.md) |
| Does DML add precision in the field trials? | On the same 83,319 water-trial households, fully interacted regression and ridge DML gave SEs of **0.1666 and 0.1690** thousand gallons. On the same 229,444 turnout-trial voters, the household-clustered SEs were **0.3225 and 0.3230** percentage points. | **Prefer ANCOVA with pre-treatment predictors** and the variance appropriate to assignment. Both experiments support adjustment. A single realized SE does not establish repeated-sampling coverage or relative bias. [Trial comparisons and timing](empirical.md) |
| Did forward validation overturn the Italian bank result? | On 39 scored quarters the forward ridge GDP response was **−0.018%**. Removing three calendar-invalid scores with every fitted model fixed gave **−0.127%**. On 36 valid quarters, a forward ridge fit with disjoint raw-data windows gave **−0.030%**, compared with **−0.026%** for a conventional six-lag-control local projection. Both intervals include zero. | **Use DML as a sensitivity** to the shock specification, calendar, fitting direction and controls. The published estimate reproduces. There is one macro trajectory, few dates and no observed causal truth against which to rank these estimates. [Italian comparison on common dates](empirical.md) |
| Does forward estimation forecast later responses? | Across seven GSS waves, effect-forecast RMSEs were **3.44 pp** for full history, **3.45** for weighted standardized OLS, and **3.47** for cross-fitted ridge AIPW. All three missed the 2024 decline. In four reminder cohorts, zero had the smallest point loss; the jointly refitted DML versus half-history comparison was unresolved. | **Prefer historical and zero benchmarks for transport.** Test any improvement across genuinely new assignments. A pooled model trained in earlier cricket seasons reduced the 2025 within-season SE from 3.020 to 2.765 pp. This is evidence of precision for estimating a current effect; the separate annual forecast exercise gave no clear DML advantage. [Transport checks](transport.md) · [Reminder intervals](../prospective/development.md) |
| Can better prediction compensate for poor identification? | On the planted bike-share null, temporal ridge PLR estimated **−153.47** rentals on average while the known-assignment estimator's bias was **−3.66** on the same days. In California the key adoption fold had no prior treated years. In retail, adding brand controls moved the slope far more than switching between linear regression and ridge DML. | **Use the randomized design, donor comparison, or a defensible instrument where applicable. Withhold the causal claim when support or assignment remains unexplained.** A forward split, a small HAC SE or a reproducible coefficient cannot resolve those issues. [Application-by-application review](applications.md) |

A separate retrospective test asks which part of DML earned the simulation gain. We held the three fitted tree-model pairs fixed and changed whether each independent observation had helped train the pair used to score it. In the interaction case, RMSE was **0.0453** for held-out scores and **0.0425** for scores on training observations. The paired difference was +0.0029, with a 95% Monte Carlo interval of +0.0011 to +0.0047. The evidence favors the trees' ability to represent interactions. It supplies no independent accuracy gain from cross-fitting in these finite-sample designs. All variants use the same orthogonal score, so the comparison isolates training-set membership. The in-sample intervals also undercovered. [Cross-fitting comparison](simulation-crossfit-paired.csv) · [Design and scope](simulation.md)

## Highest-priority corrections

- **Monitor behavior.** The default scheduler requests nuisance-model retraining for a correctly specified fair randomized propensity with population prediction R² of zero. Providing zero as baseline R² can raise a division error. A tiny change from a zero effect can also raise a critical stability alert. These are reproducible problems for causal monitoring. Use design-specific diagnostics and an outcome-scale decision threshold before acting on such alerts. [Monitoring counterexamples](failures.md)
- **Panel and dynamic targets.** Demeaning X erased nonlinear adjustment information and produced a slope of 1.878 for a truth of one. In a separate randomized series, conditioning on a post-treatment state gave a lag estimate of −0.022 for a direct effect of zero; the total effect through that state was 1.6. Write down which path the intervention should include before choosing the estimator. [Matched counterexamples](failures.md)
- **401(k) interpretation.** The two extra companion controls are wealth balances measured in the survey wave, and total wealth contains the financial-assets outcome. Including them changed same-row linear OLS from **$5,896 (SE $1,524)** to **$8,247 (SE $501)**. The precision gain does not justify conditioning on outcome components for an eligibility total effect. Keep the nine-control author reproduction and IV participation analysis distinct. [Source definitions and fits](failures.md)

## How to use this review

The [36-row study inventory](applications.md) gives a recommended method, target population, assignment or identifying assumption, timing and evidence for each application. The [ten-chapter review](manuscript.md) covers the manuscript and software, including synthetic insurance, the scalar sensitivity curve, heterogeneous effects, vintage-aware prediction, and decisions made by the monitoring utilities. [Matched simulations](simulation.md), [field-trial and macro replays](empirical.md), [mechanism and implementation probes](failures.md) and [transport comparisons](transport.md) each include their inputs, reproduction command and results.

For an effect in an observed sample, assess bias and interval coverage when truth is known, or compare design-consistent estimates and their uncertainty when it is unknown. For a future effect, freeze competing predictions and score them against the same later experiment. Report common-row comparisons before changing the training or evaluation population. Count independent datasets, trials, or time origins at the level that actually varies; shared people, shocks and histories induce dependence. All newly selected comparisons here are retrospective. The published cricket and GSS forecast records retain their original digests. [Prospective records](../prospective/README.md)
